1 · The arena: orbit space ESTABLISHED

Every physically distinct gluon-field configuration is one point in this space. It has an interior — a curved shape — and a boundary: the Gribov wall, the one place the theory is forced to mint a scale.

The shape's famous numbers — K·V = 0.0801 and tr-ratio = 0.8158 — turn out to be the values of pure randomness (Haar dice): real dynamics shifts them by under 1%. And the interior is provably blind to the wall: our probe correlation measured r = 0.009. MEASURED BY US
